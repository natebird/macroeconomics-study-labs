The question behind the whole unit

Units 3 and 4 asked what fiscal and monetary policy do right away. Unit 5 asks what happens after the economy adjusts. For almost every question in this unit, answer two things: the short-run effect, and the long-run effect once wages and expectations catch up.

The graphs use simplified teaching models. The direction of every change matches the AP Macroeconomics course framework, but the exact numbers are illustrative, not real data.
